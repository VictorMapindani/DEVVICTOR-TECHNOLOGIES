# 1. Executive Summary

DevVictor Technologies is a software development and technology services business focused on designing practical, reliable, and maintainable digital solutions.

The business will provide software development services to individuals, startups, small and medium-sized businesses, organizations, and other clients that require websites, web applications, business systems, automation tools, and customized digital solutions.

The DevVictor Technologies Platform will serve as both the company’s public digital presence and a business support system. It will showcase technical capabilities, explain available services, display completed projects, collect client inquiries, provide preliminary project estimates, and support future client and project management.

The business will initially focus on building a strong portfolio, developing practical experience, establishing professional credibility, and acquiring suitable client projects. Services and internal business capabilities will expand as technical experience, client demand, and operational capacity grow.

# 3. Problem Statement

Many individuals and businesses require digital systems but may experience challenges such as:

- Limited access to suitable software development services.
- Difficulty understanding which type of system they need.
- Unclear software development costs.
- Uncertainty about project timelines.
- Poor communication during software development.
- Reliance on manual or inefficient business processes.
- Difficulty finding software solutions suited to their specific requirements.

DevVictor Technologies aims to address these challenges by providing clear service information, structured project discovery, transparent preliminary estimates, professional communication, and customized software solutions.

# 8. Revenue Model

The initial revenue model may include:

## 8.1 Fixed-Price Projects

A project price is agreed upon after the project requirements, scope, deliverables, and timeline are understood.

This model may be suitable when requirements are sufficiently clear.

## 8.2 Milestone-Based Projects

The project is divided into agreed milestones, with payments linked to completed and accepted work.

Possible milestones may include:

1. Requirements and planning.
2. Design.
3. Initial development.
4. Feature completion.
5. Testing and deployment.

## 8.3 Hourly or Time-Based Work

This model may be used for:

- Maintenance.
- Technical support.
- Small changes.
- Troubleshooting.
- Consulting.

## 8.4 Recurring Support Plans

Clients may pay recurring fees for:

- Maintenance.
- Monitoring.
- Updates.
- Technical support.
- Small ongoing improvements.

## 8.5 Future Product Revenue

Future revenue may include:

- Subscription-based software products.
- Software-as-a-Service products.
- Industry-specific digital platforms.
- Reusable software products.

No prices are defined in this document because pricing should be based on validated business costs, project scope, market conditions, client requirements, and operational capacity.

# 14. Key Risks

| Risk | Potential Impact | Initial Response |
|---|---|---|
| Limited initial portfolio | Reduced client confidence | Build high-quality demonstrable projects |
| Inconsistent development | Delayed platform delivery | Use small milestones and scheduled work |
| Unclear client requirements | Scope changes and rework | Use structured discovery and written requirements |
| Underestimating projects | Financial or schedule loss | Break work into features and estimate carefully |
| Scope creep | Delays and increased cost | Define scope and manage change requests |
| Technical skill gaps | Slower delivery | Use focused learning and incremental implementation |
| Limited client acquisition | Low revenue | Use multiple acquisition channels |
| Security issues | Client and business risk | Apply secure development practices |
| Overdependence on one person | Capacity limitations | Document processes and plan sustainable growth |
